New Beginning

 Today I read after a long gap. I read just one chapter, maybe 10 pages just. I am still happy, as it is better than reading nothing. I am reading “Sister of my Heart”. Today’s chapter was from Sudha’s lens. Both Anju and Sudha are pregnant. It’s really fascinating to know how the starting of motherhood changes a woman, changes her surroundings. How from the day you become pregnant, the identity of a woman changes. Can she be the woman just the day before, someday in her life! Sometimes I just wonder. As I am growing up, if I try to picture myself in the situation, a fear grows inside me. I can’t imagine further. As in Sudha’s case, the love and care she is getting is kind of suffocating her. That it is compelling her to think if all these love is for the bearer of their grandchildren. Maybe it is. Maybe the extra care is always for the children to come. The mother loses her identity at the very moment she becomes pregnant. The strange thing is the woman itself is happier and anyone else to bring that child to this world. To bring a piece of her to life. 

Maybe now I am writing things I am not supposed to write in this blog. These things are better suited for my diary. Just beginning my journey to writing things about me reading.
