what if?

 i was busy the whole day today driving, yesterday was full of guests at home. today i picked the next story of heart lamp; the shroud. i did not know the meaning of the word, and learnt one. i liked the story and the plot of it. familiar setting, familiar everything. in between i lost my concentration, yet i could manage to read it full, actually twice (the next time i read to ensure the complete message of the story). i liked one narration where the one of the main character feels that the currency notes of the poors are dirtier even if you give them the clean ones. i have observed this and thought about it and the inclusion of this in the story instantly caught my mind. the entire story is actually the one I had thought of writing one 4 years ago, i started the first paragraph, but could never complete it. the narration and the environment will defnitely be different. but the core idea is actually pretty similar. this i have noticed in several other scenarios as well (like imaginging a music video shoot, where the idea matches with somebody else already had done which i have not yet watched),. this actually boosts my confidence, my thughts, observations are not something that anybody can ignore. 


-- Male Donkey
