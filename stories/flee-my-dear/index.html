flee my dear

was a lazy sunday, had enough of my time, so i decided to watch a movie as I was also feeling a bit lethargic. Thappad is a bollywood movie ft tapsi pannu and some other actors whom I know by face, not by name. I had watched the trailer and also by the title of the movie, it was evident what it is going to be. i like how the director was trying to make the audience familiar to the common day-to-day routine tasks the character have to do. it was apparaent. i havenot watched lots of tapsee pannu's movies, however, i understood she is a talented actor who can make you believe that she can portray different character at ease. i often find it really funny when you see somebody in movie and you take it in your mind that in their personal life too they will be somewhat similar to that. but which is not true coz they are indeed so naturally talented actor that you get this belief installed in your head after seeing their performance. in this movie Pannu is a housewife, she looks really pretty. i feel pity for the husband who would ever let such woman go away from their life. one thing which makes the movie brilliant is i could not exactly predict the ending. as a typical bollywood movie, this should rather have a happy ending. however, the movie features a happy ending only, but in a realistic way. it does not feature a bollywood kind of song and dance, rather as a background music (only one song). overall, i would like to recommend this, however if you are emotionally loaded person, better to avoid.
