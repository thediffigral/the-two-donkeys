the well deserved

well, the last chapter of Urvashi's book - memory.. i feel the chapter serves the purpose of conclusion of a long article. i feel the idea of putting it nearly similar to the introduction chapter of the book. the author was a lot skeptical about doing the book, becuase the amount of strength required to face the subject is not easy as she predicted. Digging somebdoy's memoery for a bitter experience is not even what I'd do or would ever consider doing. The entire subject matter if we see, is full of sadness, measury and what not - i feel really sad to say, but the word 'negativity' has to be the only word that can summarise the picture. yes, reading about some incident which you have not gone thorough - you can be sad, but not sadder than the one who has lived through it. the author however ended the chapter with two 'positive' stories which eventually refreshen my mind. i think it will do it for everyone reading it. the cross border friendship is what will bring the finest wave of relaxation amidst all the tense description. maybe, it is the happy ending after all the disaster, after all the chaos - you still find in it - the little bit humanity, little bit of understanding, little bit of hope still remains.. and that's how the world still habitual. 

I owe so much to the author. althoguh i was not at all familiar with the partition when i was in school, (leave my college days, i was really dumb history wise, only because i studied for exam purpose - i still think of going back to school where i will begin to learn everything only to learn, not for any exam), i came to know about it when i read Anchal Malhotra's book. This book was however, when i took to read, i began with the familiar incidents. but then gradually i realised the science of the book is different. i learnt to think different dimensions of the topic, or even i think i can now figure out how the scholar persons do the research in humanities. i will recommend this book to everyone out there who wants to do a thorough research on topics like this where everything can be described logically, yet the approach to tackle the question will require more emotioinal intelligence thatn intelligence. I learnt how to ask different questions on social topics like this, which is actually all very important for any common citizen to ask, to consider in day-to-day lives. with all the hearts to the author, i gratefully acknowledge Urvashi Butalia for this masterpiece. Thank you. 


-- Male Donkey.
