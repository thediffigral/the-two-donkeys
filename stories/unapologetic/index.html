Written on 2026-09-28, after going through Madhupur Bohudur.

unapologetic

Recently I went to a bookfair and bought Modhupur Bohudur by Xilobhodro. It's a pen name of Reboti Mohan Dutta Choudhury, one of the celebrated litterature, won the Sahitya Academy for the very same book. Since childhood, for the general knowledge subject, we have been taught the list of Sahitya academy award winners and memorize them. The Madhupur Bohudur is one such whom we remembered. Interestingly I share the same year of birth with the book being awared the prize. so i instantly decided to buy without any second thought. 

The book is a collection of short stories. Modhupur is possibly the author's hometown. the first story, with the same name of the book, is a 38 page long. While i could sense the author mostly portrayed that time's story and people, i could not connect much of it. I read on. the subsequent stories are not that long, some are 3 pages, some are 5. many stories after I went through, felt like did it have to stop it this way? i mean it is not that i remained curious, it is more about 'oh? has it ended already? i thought there will be more on it'. For example i particular story i now can recall where he is not meeting one of his friends for many days, even in close encounter. this is because his friend had grown long beard. that was the story. sure it can have bigger interpretation, but as a general reader, alright, the author could tried to mean that it is the physical appearance we care more, but what about the mental connection, we don't care about meeting people once they are not in our favor or required etc etc, but other than that i could not think about any more and neither I felt like doing so. there were similar such stories and possibly i could not be able to extract the actual juice out of it - i can admit that too. his writing style felt like more of Saurabh Kumar Chalihas- one of the greatest writers. In the case of Chaliha too, in some stories i feel like i could not connect to what he actually tried to portray through his words (exceptions are there, for example, the story 'geometry' - I like it). Maybe authors like Dutta Choudhury and Chaliha are too cryptic to express their feelings as academically both of them are Maths and Physics professors respectively. paritcularly with Dutta Choudhury, i had this suspection that behind the stories there may be messages which are far beyond our understandings and because of this set prior in my mind, i could not get rid of it. i thought of giving it a read again, but yet not done. i wanted to understand for sure. 


-- Male Donkey.
