thrilling experiment

I am a bit occupied with one of my research problems and my head is quite busy calculating how to figure a way out. So inherently i go for some light headed things. At such time amidst such head heavy times, i find it hard to concentrate on topics that require substantial amount of concentration. Hoping that, and also to get a bit relaxation while I also enjoy the subject matter, I pick up the novel Kokadeutar Har  by Nabakanta Baruh. Quite young we used to remember the awards author and title of book, and this book was a known since then. However, never have I ever thought of reading it once. Recently I saw a social media post regarding this book and thought of giving it a shot. I have not experimented myself with a lot of authors (by this I mean I don't go by author first, book later. first i find the book, and then follow it). Recently though I have been considering this and thinking of reading books from different authors whom I have known. So, I ordered the book.

it's a novel, and according to the social media post, it is one of best thriller Assamese literature ever have. Indeed the first two chapters have grabbed my attention already. It's a small one and so probably I can finish it soon. Nonetheless, i think i will lengthen the duration. 

-- Male Donkey.
