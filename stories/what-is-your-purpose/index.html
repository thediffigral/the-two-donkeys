what is your purpose?

i am astouned to see my limited knowledge and thought process/capacity to think beyond when I started to read the margin - the next chapter of Urvashi Butalia's book. When I looked at the title of the chapter post ending of the previous chapter, i could not think what is the chapter going to be. I thought some boundary related may be, but I could never thought about the marginalized people. I knew about it when I had read little economics, but i did not expect. this is the limitation i carry in my brain, and i am so good to find the limited capacity it posses. However, even the author had some pre-stored knowledge which is not the truth in reality. the horizon people, even i thought of them as hindu. but they are not. they are the horizons (pronounced hor-e-jon, not hor- eye-zon).  and because of that identity, they were saved. they were not attacked, not even the males, not the women-child, nobody. this is rather a surprising fact atleast for me. what religion can take you to, religion can take your life, and not possessing it can also save your life - this is remarkable. i salute for the discovery of this 'degrees of freedom' by the author. never in my earlier knowledge on the partition of india had i come to know this. this chapter is genuinely the most 'if it is not included in the book, then the book is incomplete' kind of part of the book. and indeed, like the author has acknowledged, the marginalized people are those people, without whom our regular day-to-day life is impossible to live-by, however, they are often the mistreated one. 

oh i also figured out the oral history thing butalia is employing. the chapter begins with the topic at hand, and they she adds portion of an interview specifically taken for that subject matter. the interview is like the research result we have. she explains the result, and for that some poriton of the result is added whenever is required. explains mean you may take it as the discussion part. and only to the end, she reveals the complete raw result (the interview transcript). now i am thinking about this, if she had included the interview transcript in the beginning, probably different people have interpreted it differently (even now anybody can do it). but adding it to the end makes me get inclined to what already has been discussed in the chapter. this is an unnecessary thought but i thought should be included here.
