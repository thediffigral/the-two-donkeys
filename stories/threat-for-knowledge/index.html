threat for knowledge

I was so surprised to know that even a researcher can get anonymous letter on the research progress they were doing and what not to do, what to do. at this point, i understood why venki is writing a book - becuase his journey into finding answer to a fundamental question is not just limited to extraction of new knowledge, it also features an adventurous  journey overall. this chapter has their structure developed and they wrote it to Nature. while their rivalry group Ada had also wrote to Nature but were rejected for showing not much progress over the last published paper of venki's. i could see the author's sensitivity regarding the manuscript, because he, this time, hand-delivered the draft to Nature's London office. the progress of the story (i would say the journey) has become quite interesting now.
