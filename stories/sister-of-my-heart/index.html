Sister of my Heart

 Yesterday night I finished reading the book "Sister of my Heart" by Chitra Banerjee Divakaruni. It took me more than four months to read this book. Not like I am not liking the book or something. I was loving it from the beginning. Although I took four months to finish it, I still consider it as an achievement. My brain is rotted to a level that I start books, I find it interesting, I read it for few days, then I leave it and completely forget about it. Then I pick up a new book after one or two months. Then the cycle repeats. This writing about what we learn from reading can be  a way to break this cycle. I am hopeful and enthusiastic about it. Anyway, the book, Chitra Banerjee has written the stories of two sisters; two sisters connected by souls. Two sisters brought up in a household with only women. Although the story was set up in late 80s, I could still relate. I myself is grown up with my sister, in our childhood we also shared a similar kind of bond like Anju and Sudha (maybe we are no longer the sisters of our hearts like Anju and Sudha are till the end of the story). The big dreams that Anju had from her childhood, and the softness of Sudha, remind me of exactly how me and my sister were. The rebellious Anju; Anju inspiring Sudha to be brave, and Sudha supporting Anju in all her adventures. Certainly the circumstances were completely different for me and my sister from that of Anju and Sudha. Chitra Banerjee has picked two women characters, one smart, intelligent, firecious and rebel; and another sweet, beautiful and obedient or I can say the type our society expects a girl/women to be. I am amazed how beautifully she has shaped the women characters. At the beginning of the book, Sudha is the quiet girl, it is expected from her that she is weak, she will behave exactly what the society expects a women to behave. She is portrayed like this, in most of the story she is shown to made sacrifices, for Anju, for their family's reputation.  She is shown to kind of living her life for others (its the exact way most of the women still live their lives, the scenario is changing, that makes me happy sometimes). As the story unfolds her character grows, she became stronger, it give me immense pleasure to see Sudha make the right choice at the end. Sudha choosing herself and her daughter over family's reputation, breaking out from the cage of her in laws house. It give me more happiness to see how the mothers welcome her decision and support her. Women standing for women. I was so happy to see the mothers following a modern lifestyle after that incident. They also broke the cage of their old beliefs and the burden of the fake pride that the society has put on them as the widows of the "chatterjee"s.  

Anju was the one I can mostly relate from the beginning of the book. She was the straightforward, rebellious one from the begging. It hurts me when they had to marry, leaving their dream of higher studies. Then she went to USA. It is strange how leaving so far apart from each other, with all the difficulties/jealousy and everything they had to go though, they still remained each others pillar of support. Initially, I was more drawn towards Anju, as at her childhood part I discovered herself to have the same vibe as mine. But then I fell in love with Sudha's character. Maybe I still resonate more with Anju, but how Sudha evolved was what give me immense joy. Anju had her own battle to fight. She can also be a true inspiration for many women out there. How throughout her life she has shown courage, she is a fighter. I was so sad when she lost "prem" from overworking to save money to support Sudha. How much she loves Sudha that she worked to that extent. I wanted to know more about their life, what will happen in America, can Anju and Sudha begin their boutique. Will Sudha come back and give Ashok another chance. But Chitra banerjee has ended it in a very sweet spot. It made me craving more. Actually, I have a habit, in movies and books, I always keep searching for myself. And when I find a piece of myself, I instantly fall in love. In this book I found it. I am happy that I got it as a gift and read it.
