morning blues

 i have watched a movie recently, the odyssey. i was tired the all day, could feel the low energy the time we decided to watch, that too after a heavy meal full of pork, whiskey and boiled vegetables and lots of rice. was travelling by car almost the entire 90% of the daylight, so naturally i was not that enthusiastic about the movie, that too with the preassumption that it's a Nolan directed movie, will I ever be able to understand it completely in a single watch? however, i had to follow, only with the intention in mind that i will fall asleep. however, once it began, i could not but watch it with so full of concetration that i literally announced to my buddies that it was the best ever decision in the entire day. the movie's speed is really admirable. a nonlinear way of telling a story visually is not an easy task, and that too an adaption of an epic highly praised and anybody can question if you dare to mis-treat anything. the movie hall also had a good number of feature (not IMAX) that could bring the intended feel of the drama. the score, the cinematography, the direction, eveything i feel had a class that is beyond the present. whereas similar stories in India, people makes it a god, in this case, the hero is not made God. That is why even when i had an objection when the hero (odysemus) shots the arrows in the final act to the enemies surrounding him (i mean the way he shot was really not his character should do),  i feel, that could also mean he was not god, he's just a human who has lost the initial energy he had during his peak time. this is not a thing anybody should question by the way. i also liked the scene where pelenomous (heros wife) was approached by a man who has the hunger for the throne and her love (probably), where both of them was behind a cloth pelenomous was weaving..  i assumed the camera was this side of the cloth to show the no-clarity of things b/w them. however, in the last scene, where pelenomous was talking to a begger (who is supposed to be odyssemus not yet revelead his identity yet), both of them were on the two different side, with odyssemus clearly speaking his guilt he had. Once pelenomous knew (i suppose,) the begger is the one, she removed the barrier b/w them, and announced the 'swyomobor' where she will pick her potential new husband if anyone can strung the bow and shot through a rally of axes. this was my interpretation only, i had to read the epic text. 

this is the thing i achieved, that i have to read the book. by the way, my sleep was gone, and we could wake up till 4 in the moring.  we had to, for obvious reasons. (;
