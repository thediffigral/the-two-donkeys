the rivalry

With the hype I myself took right after watching the trailer of NRC-Nalparar RamChandra, I wanted to watch the very first show of the first day. I waited however, and I just failed to get one of the rarest instances in the world, the worth of the hype I took in the very first show could have been more unique. The interesting thing is, i still could feel it even if I failed to do so. what a worth of investment of time and energy into this movie- i could only thank myself for taking the decision to go and watch it without any biasedness or somebody's appeal, 'go watch this new movie, it's really good'. Instead, I have literally told many of my friends, even to many whom I don't expect that they will go and watch an assamese movie. To my surprise, with the enthusiasm I showed probably- they went ahead and thanked me for the recommendation. 

the movie is super top notch that I still am trying to find out major flaws. there have been none i could figure out. The funny thing is i kept on wondering and the 1st half of the movie ended, and it went like a cozy bike ride in a road with yellow paddy field sideways, with your partner behind and with no sign of un-peace. I literally tried to find what can they do a mistake, even slightly in the first half, but the execution was so good that i had to leave the thought. it will discard your every guess and will keep checking your confidence on it. i lost my intellectual battle for a movie to the team behind the movie. i envy all of them, how can they think so good?

after coming back from hall, i have discussed and pushed many of my friends. one of my friends correctly said (he has watched the movie) that this movie actually has made us unlearn our previous tastes. to be honest, i literally have failed to recall where did i get such feel after watching an assamese movie. this is so rare. definitely some minor issues, flaws will be there, but those are the proof it is human made, driven by human emotion. i feel ashemed with the fact that with the certain amount of overconfidence i considered the writer of the movie to be a very average youtuber. he is not, atleast this movie is his finest creation. i don't know what he will do eventually, and i will be happy if he prove me wrong everytime. this product is actually somethings so rooted, something like, some south indian movies depict their life so well that we want to go and stay there - it gives you exactly that. the comic timing, the sadness, the romance- oh just i remember, the beginning of the romance is what resemblance to my idea (i mean part of the scence or expressions i want to portray)

this bar is high now. let's see, what is next!
