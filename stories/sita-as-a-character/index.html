Sita as a character !

As I am reading the story of Sita through the lens of Chitra Banerjee, I am deeply in love with Sita. From the childhood, I was always attracted to the "Mahabharata" more than the "Ramayana". It may be due to the environment in which I was grown up. Its in our tradition to utter "Krishna" in everything. So, by the time I can understand anything about God, spirituality and religion, I always considered 'Krishna' to be the one God. This has not changed even after I have grown up. Now, I know both are the incarnation of the same God, but yet when I have to visualise God, its always "Krishna". One more thing, a far as I can remember, I have always questioned about Ram abandoning "Sita" at the end, even in my childhood. When I was reading and trying to recollect how and what ideas about Sita I hold form my childhood, I am recalling the stories my maternal grandmother told us. She was a great storyteller, I can still remember those nights, she used to tell stories to us. Me, ba, Aaijoni and Pallab sitting around her, demanding her to tell the same stories again and again. There is an Assamese novel "Bideh Nandini" (I thing I have remembered it correctly), by Dr. Malini. And it was featured in the Sunday magazine "Purbachal" by "amar axom" as a serial (I do not know the word for it, if it has any).  Aaita used to keep waiting to read it, once she read, when she meet us she will then tell the story in her own way. And she used to give it a new version (that I have imagined, because I have never read that novel, but the interpretations were so coded to Aaita herself that I now think it may be so) and tell it. She has mostly told the stories after Sita has been kidnapped by Ravana, and she was kept in the cage. I remember how Aaita would feel pity for Sita, how sometimes telling the story she will almost break out crying. And I somehow developed an affection for Sita. At the end when Ram rescued Sita and then questioned her purity, I was angry and the little mind of mine would rebel to accept Ram as a God. And I was even more angry at Ram when I have learnt the fate of Sita, the story about "fat mela boxumati patale lukao". I remember me debating about Ram being a God with my grandmother and grandfather. Though I have this sweet corner, I always looked upto Krishna, Arjun and Draupadi, never to Ram and Sita. Now, this book of Chitra Banerjee has make me feel deeply about Ram and Sita. As I am still to reach the part with Ravana in this book, I am touched by the love between Sita and Ram. How wonderful of a woman Sita is! How beautifully she embraces the flaws of Ram. Sometimes reading the book I question myself, shouldn't I learn this from my beloved character! I definitely should, and I would try my best.
