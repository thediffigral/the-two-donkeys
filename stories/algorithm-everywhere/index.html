 Today I picked up a new book to read “The days at the morisaki bookshop”. I have bought this book long back. Maybe at the beginning of 2025. Once I even picked up the book to read it, but somehow I never did. I always had a fascination about books and bookstores. This book was kind of very famous in somewhere around 2024. I saw the book, and somehow I painted the idea in my mind that it will be about someone who will regularly visit the morosaki bookshop. She/he ( although I mainly assume the main character to be a girl) will meet another visitor and will fall in love ( I have an absurd habit of assuming things a priori, its a reflection of the same). But, it’s not certainly like that, the protagonist is someone who is kind of working in the morisaki bookshop. The most beautiful thing I can imagine while reading this book is the location of the morisaki bookshop. It is located in a place which according to the book used to be the largest destination for secondhand books. Rows and rows of bookstores in both side of the road, and some little cafes and bars in between. It’s nothing like a heaven for someone who likes books ( I would definitely love to live in such a neighbourhood). I am very interested to know how the story takes turn and how the morisaki bookshop will change Takako’s life. 

One more interesting thing that caught my mind while reading was that, the protagonist Takako is falling in love with reading books just in the story. From being a girl who sleeps all the extra tome she got, she built the habit of reading of books ( she literally read from night till morning to finish a book that single night. that was some tale of very long past for me). That’s exactly what we are trying to do here. Nowadays, all the social media platforms saw us things we think about, talk about. We see contents very relatable to what’s happening in our life. It’s interesting that the book I pick up to read also happens to do the same thing (algorithm worked here to, lol).
