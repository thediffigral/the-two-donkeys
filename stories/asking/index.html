Finally, i am starting again, with the book 'a little history of philosophy'. wanted to buy it, however costs a lot. the pdf is easy to find, so it is not a problem. i liked the book instantly with the first chapter, as it tells many things which I also believe to be true. the chapter features Socrates and his disciple Plato.. the intriguing thing is that the author makes the reader that was it really Socrates or just a imaginary piece by Plato. just merely knowing things to do or gathering information is not the goal, what makes us human is when we truly understand the reason of being. Socrates asks silly questions and liked it when he baffles others by counter examples to their arguments or any replies. even now when i was reading the chapter, i thought of like how the words when we read makes us understand what the information is being passed on by the author - the process is quite fascinating if you think about it. like the mind is thinking something and we are expressing it directly throught speeches and expressions - and the other receiving party is understanding a copy of it - this is really a interesting process. we will only value it when we will be meeting aliens.
