Can I read on a bus !

It’s been a long gap, again. Being consistent is the last thing I can do in life. I hope I will be able to overcome this one day. I am travelling to Kaziranga today. To have an meet up with my friends. On my way from university to Dolabari, it suddenly hit me that I could have read the book on my way, maybe the journey will become little less boring ( i generally donot enjoy it when I need to travel by an ultra bus, to be brutally honest I hate ultra buses). I downloaded the epub version of the book I am reading, preparing myself for having a new experience. It’s indeed. I never thought before that it can be something I can do while travelling specially on an ultra bus. I always pick up the phone and end up scrolling through social media. Then I started reading, with kaavish playing on my ears. I liked it, as I could be entirely myself without fearing about what’s happening in the surroundings. I was totally lost in my own world. Then gradually my started a protest against me. I started feeling discomfort in reading the words, and so I lost the concentration. As I was having a tug of war between my discomfort and mu interest in the story, I missed my physical copy (anyway I believe, the small screen of your phone is not a perfect device to read books). But, indeed I enjoyed that new experience.

I probably read just one chapter. Once momoko mentioned that you can seduce any men with your food. Never in my life I imagined that can be somebody a men can find sexy. I am a lazy cook like Takako, now if that’s true I can get a new inspiration to accelerate my learning in cooking (although I never advocate for doing something only to impress men, lol ).
