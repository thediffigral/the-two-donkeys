Writing Fatigue!

 So, I feel very lazy to write. I am reading the forest of enchantments, kind of regularly. But I am not writing my blogs. Male Donkey will be kind of upset about it I guess. But, I am happy for the fact that although writing still feels like a lot of work, I am gradually building the habit of reading. Now a days, when I am alone in my room, I feel the urge to read the book instead of jut doom scrolling. And interestingly enough, when I read, sometimes in the background I think I will write this and that in the blog, and the writing part generally is not happening in the last week. I was eagerly waiting for the part when sita will be kidnapped ( abduction is the correct word as I know now ) by Ravana, and her life in Lanka. I know a part of how she sacrificed her comfort to protect her dignity and the love for Rama. But, how Chitra Banerjee is describing this as what Sita's mental state then, it was what I wanted to know. She rather made that part pretty short ( this is what I feel). Somehow I was waiting for that part and I assumed that most of the weightage   of the book should be on that. That I assumed because I wanted to know that part more. But obviously, that is  less than one year. But, I am still unable to believe what that part has unveiled about Sita's origin. I felt an strong urge to enquire about the correctness of the story, but somehow I am even lazy enough to do that. How can I so easily believe it, when Sita herself is finding it very difficult to believe and more accurately I should tell accept. 

One thing I though of writing while reading was the part with Surpanakha. How Sita felt insecure observing her offering her love to Ram ! How a human Sita is! And afterwards she too felt pity for Lakshman and Ram's reaction to Surpanakha insulting and attacking Sita. This story I was waiting to read, as I heard from some people that many feminist people often got offended by Surpanakah's fate, as they believe just offering her love to some male shouldnot be an offence. That I agree. But it is not why she was being punished. She went beyond what she should if she would have been righteous by trying to attack Sita. There she commited a crime, however, I too feel something similar to the same line as Sita, the punishment could have been something lighter. 

This book being an epic poem, is teaching me many ways of love. However, in todays world many of it would be too extreme for someone to obey, but the underlying moral is something we all can get inspiration of. And some little stories, where Sita behave completely like a normal girl, who is in love unreasonable and demanding. And Ram would fullfill her wishes although he knows how unnecessary those  are just to make her happy. 

Another thing I wanted to write is the parallels between Odyssey and Ramayana. After the similarity of the Haradhanu with the Arrow of Odysseus, it's the e jeus law, described heavily in Odyssey. This same law was a tradition of Mithila as well. And it is the jeus law, which made Sita go beyond the Laxman Rekah and being abducted my Ravan.

While I am writing now, one thought passed me. I donot even know the correct meaning of enchantment. I have heard "enchanting the mantras", but what is actually means. Does it just mean reciting holly things or it has some other meaning. This word is used in some places in the book also, I have a rough idea of what is so, I can understand what the line is trying to tell me, but yeah I should look up the meaning of it.
