Neverland !

 I started reading the book "Neverland: Zero Hour" by Rita Chowdhury. It is supposed to be part of a trilogy Neverland, and it is the first book. I bought this book on 2024 in InCignis. Rita Choudhury used to be my favourite author, maybe because the first novel I read was the book "Deu Langkhui" ( I do not know if I am writing correctly"  by her. I was in class 6 or 7. Before that I only had exposure to newspaper stories and the novels published in magazines. Both me and my sister was so amazed by the novel that, we started reading each and every novel by Rita Choudhury. She indeed is a good writer, and I have not read many of the old and polished Assamese books, comparing among the contemporary writers, she stood as my favourite. It was her new book, and without a second thought, when I saw it in a bookstall I bought the book, without even seeing the context of the book. Even till today, I did not know what it is about. Well, it is about the 1971 Bangladesh war. The author claims that it is based on true incidents, and she has done a research and collected evidences and mix it with the little imagination needed to make it a fiction. One interesting fact about the book is that, in between the text, Choudhury has added some Qr codes that we can scan and there she attached linked to some audio, and footages related to the true events, so that one can feel it. I donot know, if they are actual clips from that time or they are just recrested for the purpose of the book. I really found it interesting. Today, I just read one chapter, and it was just to build the character, I am not yet getting a glimpse of what it is going to be like. I even have no idea what neverland means, and am too lazy to even google. it. But, indeed, I am excited  to take up this journey.
