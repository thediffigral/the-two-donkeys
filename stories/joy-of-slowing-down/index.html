Joy of slowing down

I just have one more chapter left to read of 'Days at the Morisaki Bookshop'. I thought I will write after I finish (I am hpoing to finish it by tonight). But then I thought, if somehow I don't get time today then I will eventually be not writing today. Today the weather is extremely hot, after I read last night, I had just few pages to finsh the book and I was getting the urge to finish it. I picked up the book just after lunch, I was felling terribly hot, lethrgic and very weak (maybe due to dehydration). Initially, I couldnot concentrate, some things I need to revisit after reading one or two paragraphs, however, the thrust to finish kept me going. Gradually, I was indulged in the reading process and I could concentrate. As I wrote earlier that I am liking Momoko's character, the author (It is embarassing that I actually donot remember the name of the author, it is a translated book, the original was in Japanese, I need to remember the author as I liked his creation) has made Momoko's character many qualities I would like to posses. The bond between Momoko and Takako is something the author has developed very gracefully. But, how it turns out at the end, I never expected that. I still couldnot believe if what I understood is what the author intented to mean. I think I need to reread that paragraph, where this was described to get a clear picture. The conversations between them afterwards gave me the feeling that I may misunderstood something. Anyway I have just last chapter left maybe 5 or 6 pages. I wanted to finish it and then come to the lab, but then I thought it is more important to maintian the discipline to come to the lab on time. So, I left that one chapter, as I was not intending to hurry. I want to finsih it with comfort and ease, so that after I just I finish I can have a ample time for myself to devote to think about the whole book and lost in the world the author has created. That give me some kind of joy. If I finish a book in a rush, I feel so mechanical.
