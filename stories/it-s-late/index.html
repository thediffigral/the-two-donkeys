it's late!

On a recommendation from a movie-enthusiast (more enthusiast to do a film, rather watch to be precise) friend of mine, I watched the 8 AM metro. He asked me whether I have watched the lunchbox (starred Irrfan Khan) or not, and then told me then you will also like 8 AM metro. It has almost similar setting. He got me some expectations set already. I skipped one week however and then he asked me again whether I have watched the movie or not. Finally I decided I have to watch the movie. 

The movie has a calm appearance from the beginning. Only one actor is known to me, rest are new. The actors justfied the characters I must say. The story revolves around a house wife and a stranger and their familiarity, similarity and friendship. I could predict the story line (or maybe the 'this is actually this, so this is happening because of this - which is later revealed in the movie) and hence, I did not watch the movie in one single sitting. I watched it like I read a story book, one story a day, maybe a chapter a day of some prose. This movie does not have that explicitly, but one can definitely figure out where he can pause it for the day and watch it back. If you are not in mood, like you are in hurry, you are overwhelmed by work you just need some relaxation - then I definitely not expect you to watch this. To watch this movie, you need to have a proper setting where you are purposely keeping a movie time, with a stable setting. Otherwise there are many chances you are not going the like it or skip it. The entire paragraph can be summed up into 'you may find the movie boring if not watched with an intention'.

While I appreciate the story and presentation, the camerawork could have been a bit more polished. Definitely the story matters, but some camera movement was odd and serves no purpose. When I'm saying it serves no purpose, I want to say that my eye caught the camera movement explicitly and thereby my mind caught the movement more rather the emotion of the scene. Many scenes in the movie is explicitly a joint cut and although it depicts a flow in the story, but not visually. 

These all point can also happen because of the expectations was initially set to me. Yet, I'd still defend the technical points I have observed. Overall I would recommend the movie for many valuable lessons of human relationship and dynamics.
