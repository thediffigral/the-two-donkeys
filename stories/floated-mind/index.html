floated mind

unrest in my mind, yet the thought of reading something stroke my mind. quickly picked up the next story of heart tales; the taste of heaven. i began it slowly as many things were going on in my mind. however, i lost the track many times; not going to lie. amidst, i even thought of re-reading it from the portion where i thought i 'd left my concentration. still somewhat i could get the plot of story (although the initial guess turned out to be wrong what i thought the story'd be). the plot is simple (although probably i mis-treated the core of the story to be the typical relationship of in-lawmother and in-law daughter) and straight. i felt the length of the story to be longer than expected, but it could be coz i was just trying to fix my mind vs trying to keep the good habit and to fix the unrest through concentrating on reading. it somehow did not work today and i need to fully invest myself so that both the unrest and concentration gets the fix. 

-- Male Donkey
