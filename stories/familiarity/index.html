Written on 2026-09-23, after going through Neverland Zero Hour - Rita Choudhury.

Familiarity !

Today I read the next two chapters of the book. While reading the book, I am felling like I am watching some movie, for I have never read this type of stories, including the secret agencies, geopolitical affairs etc etc. Especially the scene where she describe the office of the Prime minister with the Raw chief coming to meet her in private, it was such an usual scene for me to watch in Bollywood detective movies.  At moments I feel like its the script of a movie I am reading. But this usually will not happen to readers, maybe in novels covering these stories also, those scenes will be very common. Thats gives me a motivation to explore more books like this one.

In these two chapters, I am getting a glimpse of the storyline. Choudhury has very successfully created the required suspense to grab the attention of the readers. The protagonist's mind always goes to an forth between reality and her past, and Choudhury brings her back to reality at exact points where you want to know what happened, but you have no way to know it, and you do not exactly know when it will be revealed. One have to read with attention, and maybe thats a way to grab the readers attention. 


---- She Donkey
